Samaya builds expert AI agents for investment professionals. I designed the product end to end, from the first query box to agents that plan, research, and report on their own.

The problem

“Can you get me ATOSS revenue in local currency from 2019 to 2024?”

  • Who is asking?An analyst or an MD
  • When?Before earnings, or after
  • Why?A model, a pitch, a client note
  • Where do they work?Sell side or buy side
  • What's the bigger task?One number or a full initiation
  • How do they like data?Local currency, fiscal or calendar

The same question means different things depending on who asks, when, and why. The product had to understand all of it.

01

Agent Studio

Describe the report you need in plain language. An agent drafts the outline, takes your edits, runs the research, and puts itself on a schedule.

02

Plan before it runs

Before any research starts, the agent shows how it understood you: the companies it detected, the time range, and the sources it will read. Every assumption can be edited.

03

Reasoning you can watch

Explore, validate, synthesize, summarize. Each stage of the agent's work is visible as it happens, so an analyst always knows where it is and why.

04

Evidence over confidence

Answers fill in cell by cell, each one cited. When the data isn't there, the table says no evidence instead of guessing.

05

Your sources, your rules

Filings, transcripts, broker research, the web, and a team's own documents. Analysts decide exactly what the agent reads, down to a single folder.

06

One place to start

Quick answers and deep agent work begin in the same input. Q&A and agents share one entry point, and it's easy to step up from a question to a recurring report.

07

Always on

Agents keep watching once the report ships. Triggers like a guidance change or a spread widening turn a one-time answer into ongoing coverage.
