Brilliant Smart Home

A set up flow to integrate all of your smart home devices.

Client

Brilliant Smart Home

My role

UI/UX Designer

Location

San Mateo, CA

Background

The Brilliant Smart Home system seamlessly integrates your lighting, doorbell, locks, music, and climate control. It is compatible with Ring, Nest, Sonos, Philips Hue, and Amazon Alexa. With countless features from these partners, the key challenge was simplifying installation into a seamless, unified experience.

My Role

As UI/UX designer, I created a full installation walkthrough that pairs the device, enables permissions,and sets up features.
